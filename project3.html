RPA Implementation for Customer Data Recapitulation

Robotic Process Automation

Project Description

This was a major project I worked on during my internship as an HRIS Intern. It was a collaborative initiative between the Business Support team (as users) and the HRIS team (as developers). As customer data volume continued to grow and manual recap processes became increasingly time-consuming, this project was designed to automate the task—streamlining the workload for users and significantly reducing processing time.

My Role

I assisted my mentor in designing and implementing the workflow into the RPA system. I was actively involved throughout the entire process, from the initial planning stage to final implementation.

Project Duration

Start: March 2024 | End: May 2024

Project Outcome

The automation system was successfully implemented in accordance with the initial design and user requirements. It was immediately adopted by users, significantly improving operational efficiency, minimizing human error, and reducing processing time.
